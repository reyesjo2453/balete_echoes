# BALETE: Echoes of the Sunken Veil — Complete Narrative Script & Story Bible

This document is the narrative canon and cutscene script specification for *Balete: Echoes of the Sunken Veil*, continuing directly from the events of the five-minute prologue in `docs/GAME_DESIGN_DOCUMENT.md`.

---

## Story Overview & Structural Arc

* **Prologue:** *The Quiapo Ambush* — Delivery → Blackout → Aswang Ambush → Crown Pull → Dante’s Van Extraction.
* **Act I:** *The Quiapo Underground & The Broken Seals* (Chapters 1–3) — Renzo learns the true nature of his courier parcel, infiltrates the corporate-corrupted black market, recruits Tala at the National Museum, and discovers Aevum Filipinas' *Ginhawa* siphon operations.
* **Act II:** *The Sunken Bastion* (Chapters 4–5) — A subterranean heist beneath Fort Santiago and the Pasig River.
* **Midpoint Twist:** The defeat of the Clockwork Tikbalang triggers Aevum’s master siphon; Renzo’s heirloom watch is revealed to be the stolen ignition key, breaking the colonial iron seal and triggering the premature onset of the Total Eclipse (*Laho*).
* **Act III:** *The Bleed & The Celestial Maw* (Chapters 6–8) — Metro Manila fractures under spiritual inundation. The party travels to the mystical heights of Mount Banahaw to recruit Mayari, reforges the ancestral chronometer, and ascends to the Sibuyan Rift for a multi-phase confrontation against CEO Aurelio Velez and the celestial dragon Bakunawa.

## Act I: The Quiapo Underground & The Broken Seals

### Chapter 1: Oil, Rain, and Old Wires

* **Setting:** Dante’s subterranean chop shop beneath the Tondo container terminals. Florescent tube lighting, diesel generators humming, rain rattling against zinc siding.
* **Gameplay Unlock:** Dante permanently joins the combat formation. Unlocks the **Kinetic Stagger Gauge** and **Dual-Command Stances**.

#### Scene 1.1: The Tondo Safehouse

* **Camera:** Medium isometric tracking as the delivery van slides to a halt inside a cavernous workshop. Steam hisses from the radiator.
* **Cutscene Beat (Comic Panels):**
* *Panel 1:* Renzo falls out of the passenger door onto concrete, vomiting rainwater, clutching his utility balisong with white knuckles.
* *Panel 2:* Dante slams the steel roll-up door shut, bolting three padlock chains wrapped in copper grounding wires.
* *Panel 3:* Dante drops a heavy first-aid kit and a bottle of gin onto a workbench.

> **DANTE:** Drink the gin. Don't pour it on the cuts unless you want to pass out. The claws carry corpse-rot.
> **RENZO:** *(Gasping, voice shaking)* What was that thing? That wasn't a rabid stray. It didn't have bones—it was made of fucking oil and smoke.
> **DANTE:** That was an Aswang, kid. A real one. Not the bedtime stories your lola told you to keep you from running around Carriedo at night. And it wasn't wild. It had a corporate telemetry tag clamped to its ear.
> **RENZO:** A tag? Who tags a demon?!
> **DANTE:** Aevum Filipinas. They run private security for the port authority and half the power grid. They don't kill spirits anymore, Renzo. They harvest them. Now show me the watch.

* **Narrative Beat:** Renzo pulls out the antique chronometer. Dante inspects it under a magnifying lamp. The gears are not brass—they are carved petrified balete wood and cold-cast iron, spinning backward without a mainspring. Dante explains that Renzo was never meant to deliver it to a buyer; his grandmother routed the parcel to him through a dummy courier route because Aevum hit squads raided her ancestral workshop three hours ago.

### Chapter 2: The Scent of Wax and Gunpowder

* **Setting:** The perimeter of Plaza Miranda and the market stalls behind Quiapo Church. Neon street banners glow against puddles: *Sari-Sari*, *San Miguel Beer*, *Anting-Anting ng Poong Nazareno*.
* **Objective:** Infiltrate Lola Rosa’s ransacked herbalist workshop and recover her ledger.

#### Scene 2.1: The Ransacked Workshop

* **Camera:** Slow dolly across broken glass, overturned dry herbs, shattered glass jars of *langis ng niyog* (coconut oil), and chalk wards wiped clean with industrial solvent.
* **Cutscene Beat:** Renzo picks up a charred prayer card. The Baybayin script across the walls has been methodically drilled out using concrete core-cutters.

> **RENZO:** She spent fifty years here. People brought her their sick kids when the clinics in Sampaloc turned them away. Why would anyone tear this place apart?
> **DANTE:** Look at the drill dust. Diamond-tipped bits. Standard issue for Aevum extraction teams. They didn't come for her money; they came for the *Susi ng Kailaliman*—the Key to the Deep Sump. They thought she had it. She didn't. She gave it to you.

* **Boss Encounter 1: Overseer Barloc & Aevum Enforcers**
* *The Boss:* An Aevum private contractor equipped with an experimental exoskeleton powered by blue glowing *Ginhawa* vials, backed by two leash-bound Corrupted Tiyanak hounds.
* *Combat Mechanics:* Barloc possesses heavy ballistic shields that negate normal physical attacks. Players must use Dante’s *Piston Smash* to shatter the shield before Renzo can exploit the exposed power conduits with elemental slashes.
* *What Renzo Learns:* Barloc’s dying suit broadcasts an automated telemetry ping to the **National Museum Conservation Wing**. Aevum’s lead researcher, Dr. Alejandro, is extracting an ancient colonial seal map from the museum's pre-colonial archives tonight.

#### Scene 3.1: The Standoff in the Gallery

* **Key Dialogue:**

> **TALA:** *(Stepping back, holding a bleeding arm, quill glowing with white ink)* Step back! The Treaty of 1598 strictly forbade excavation beneath the fortress foundation! You have no jurisdiction here!
> **AEVUM CAPTAIN:** The Department of Energy signed the lease, little girl. Heritage doesn't keep the Luzon grid online. Hand over the ledger of the Spanish Friar Seals, or we bury you under your own pottery.
> **RENZO:** *(Intervening with drawn balisong)* Hey! Real tough picking on someone who fights with a pen!

* **Boss Encounter 2: The Severed Manananggal Drone**
* *The Boss:* A hybridized horror—the upper torso of a bound female spirit grafted with carbon-fiber turbine rotors and high-voltage taser cables.
* *Combat Mechanics:* The boss hovers above the floor, immune to Dante’s melee arnis strikes. The player must use Tala’s *Hangin (Wind)* glyphs to ground the drone, creating an 8-second window for Renzo to execute Stagger attacks.
* *The Revelation:* Tala reveals that the pocket watch Renzo holds is not just a timepiece—it is an **Horological Seal Matrix**. In 1898, the last native watchmakers of Santa Cruz cooperated with the underground Katipunan to enclose the ancient spirit-lock of Intramuros inside a pocket watch, hiding it in plain sight among the colonial aristocracy.

## Act II: The Sunken Bastion & The Shattered Seal

### Chapter 4: Beneath the Pasig

* **Setting:** The subterranean storm overflow channels beneath the Pasig River. Murky, knee-deep water, Spanish brick archways intertwined with massive high-pressure hydraulic pipes painted with the Aevum corporate chevron.
* **Dungeon Mechanics:** Environmental water hazards. Dante uses his battery pack to electrify flooded sluice gates, while Tala deciphers encrypted Spanish-Baybayin relief stones to drain water levels.

#### Scene 4.1: The Campfire at the Sluice Gate

* **Gameplay Context:** Introduces the *Pakikisama* rest mechanic. The party rests at a dry maintenance platform, sharing instant coffee and canned corned beef over Dante’s portable burner.
* **Key Dialogue:**

> **RENZO:** *(Staring at the pocket watch)* I thought this city was just concrete, bad traffic, and bills I couldn't pay. I didn't ask for this. I just wanted my grandmother to pay off the pawnshop loan on my motorcycle.
> **TALA:** The city was built on top of graves, Renzo. The Spanish built Intramuros not to keep the pirates out, but to suppress what lived in the delta. When Aevum started dredging the riverbed for their geothermal-spirit plants, they woke the roots of the Balete trees.
> **DANTE:** *(Cleaning his arnis sticks)* If Aevum cracks the central vault beneath Fort Santiago, they won't just get free electricity. They'll drain the *Kailaliman* until the barrier collapses. And when the barrier breaks, Bakunawa won't stay in the ocean.

### Chapter 5: The Clockwork Bastion (The Midpoint Climax)

* **Setting:** An immense subterranean chamber directly beneath the dungeon vaults of Fort Santiago. Medieval stone battlements have been hollowed out to accommodate a multi-story refinery vat filled with luminescent teal *Ginhawa* liquid.
* **Boss Encounter 3: The Clockwork Tikbalang (Col. Valderama's Masterpiece)**
* *The Boss:* An ancient equine forest lord captured by Aevum, its skull replaced with a pressurized pneumatic piston engine and its limbs reinforced with hydraulic iron armature.
* *Phase 1:* High-speed galloping charges across the diorama. The player must use Tala’s *Lupa (Earth)* wards to trip the boss into wall collisions.
* *Phase 2 (Enrage):* Steam vents release; the Tikbalang rear-kicks with kinetic shockwaves. Renzo must enter Command Time-Dilation to evade the 360-degree radial blast.

#### Scene 5.2: The Betrayal of the Key

* **Camera:** Dynamic cinematic pan from the defeated beast up to the high observation balcony. Glass windows slide open. **CEO Aurelio Velez** stands flanked by private security, applauding slowly.

> **VELEZ:** Brilliant work, Mr. Reyes. Your grandmother possessed an infuriating sense of paranoia. She keyed the master escapement of the seal to her own bloodline. We could have spent decades trying to brute-force the frequency. But you brought it directly into the chamber.
> **RENZO:** What are you talking about? We just broke your beast!
> **VELEZ:** You broke the beast that was keeping the pressure regulator stable. Look at your hand, boy.

* **The Horror:** The pocket watch in Renzo's hand does not stop. It vibrates with violent resonance. The gears violently unwind, glowing blinding white.
* Across the chamber floor, the massive, centuries-old Spanish iron seal—inscribed with both Latin prayers and native incantations—fractures down the center.
* Deep, pitch-black water surges up from the fissure with the force of an erupting volcano. The sky above Fort Santiago literally tears open; the moon in the night sky turns deep obsidian violet.
* **The Eclipse (*Laho*) has begun weeks ahead of schedule.**
* An astral roar echoes from deep within the earth, shattering every piece of glass in a three-kilometer radius. Bakunawa has smelled the breach.
* Dante grabs Renzo by the scruff of his jacket, dragging him backward as the refinery collapses into the chasm.

## Act III: The Bleed & The Celestial Maw

### Chapter 6: The Broken Metropolis

* **Setting:** Manila in ruins. The Pasig River has overflowed with luminous astral water. MRT tracks are suspended in mid-air over purple-tinted spatial rifts. Military checkpoints are overrun by terrified spirits fleeing the subterranean depths.
* **Objective:** Escape the ruined capital and reach the sacred caldera of Mount Banahaw to reforge the shattered chronometer before the Eclipse reaches totality.

#### Scene 6.1: Crossing the Sierra Madre Foothills

* **Gameplay Unlock:** **Mayari** joins the party after an introductory boss trial. Unlocks **Shadow-Phase Traversal** (passing through spiritual energy fields and dodging directly through boss hitboxes).
* **Boss Encounter 4: Mayari, The Crescent Huntress**
* *Context:* Mayari believes Renzo and Dante are Aevum agents bringing the stolen seal to the mountains.
* *Combat Mechanics:* Rapid teleportation, illusion clones, and crescent blade flurries. Players must use Tala’s illumination glyphs to dispel her shadows before Renzo can parry her strikes.

### Chapter 7: The Forge of Mount Banahaw

* **Setting:** The crystal-clear volcanic springs and sacred grottos (*Puesto*) of Mount Banahaw. Ancient balete trees here are uncorrupted, their roots forming natural stairways over mossy basalt cliffs.
* **Gameplay Mechanic: Horological Reforging**
* Renzo does not simply receive a magical sword. At the sacred spring, he uses his watchmaker tools, Dante’s portable welding torch, Tala’s Baybayin gold leaf, and Mayari’s astral silver to completely dismantle and rebuild the pocket watch.
* *Result:* The weapon evolves into **The Celestial Chronometer (Kawal ng Liwanag Tier)**. Renzo’s balisong now permanently burns with starlit silver energy.

#### Scene 7.1: The Resolution of Loob (Inner Resolve)

* **Key Dialogue:**

> **DANTE:** If we go back down there to the Sibuyan platform, there is no extraction van. We're going straight into the belly of the beast.
> **TALA:** All our history has led to this point. If we fail, there will be no one left to write the next chapter.
> **RENZO:** My whole life, I ran from my family's debt. I thought if I kept my head down, delivered my packages, and ignored the rot in this city, I'd survive. But the rot doesn't stop because you look away. *Walang atrasan.* We finish this.

### Chapter 8: The Celestial Maw (The Climax)

* **Setting:** Aevum’s oceanic extraction platform stationed in the Sibuyan Sea directly above the deepest marine trench. The sky is total obsidian; the moon is completely eclipsed, surrounded by a blinding silver corona. Astral lightning strikes the water.

#### Scene 8.1: Confronting the Architect

* **Boss Encounter 5: Aurelio Velez, The Synthetic Avatar**
* *The Boss:* Velez has injected himself with pure, distilled *Ginhawa Prime*, his body hovering inside an ornate brass harness inspired by Spanish monstrances.
* *Combat Mechanics:* Uses all elemental affinities (*Apoy*, *Tubig*, *Lupa*, *Hangin*). Switches immunities rapidly. Party must cycle between Dante, Tala, and Mayari to strip his defensive barriers before Renzo executes Stagger strikes.
* *Velez's Dying Words:*

> **VELEZ:** *(Coughing luminescent fluid)* Fools... you think I wanted destruction? I wanted to free us! Three hundred years of Spanish priests, fifty years of Americans, decades of oligarchs... we were always weak! With Bakunawa’s heart, we would have had the power to dictate terms to the world!
> **RENZO:** You didn't want freedom, Velez. You just wanted to be the one holding the leash.

* **Final Boss Encounter: BAKUNAWA, THE SUNKEN SERPENT**
* *The Scale:* Colossal multi-arena battle taking place on the fracturing deck of the sea platform and across the astral reflection of the submerged Manila skyline.
* **Phase 1: The Leviathan's Fins:** Battle against the colossal beast’s crest and whiskers as it circles the platform, slamming tidal waves of spectral water across the arena.
* **Phase 2: The Moon's Core:** Bakunawa opens its gaping maw to consume the celestial core. The party enters the beast's spiritual throat, fighting corrupted memories of Manila.
* **Phase 3: The Final Bayanihan Climax:** The party coordinates their ultimate unified technique. Dante anchors the serpent’s jaw with industrial grounding cables; Tala binds its throat with giant luminous Baybayin chains; Mayari phases through its eye to sever its astral optic nerve; Renzo triggers the Crown of the Celestial Chronometer, freezing the beast in time for 10 seconds to drive the forged spirit-blade directly into the cracked seal on its brow.

## Story Endings & Resolution

The game features **three distinct narrative endings**, determined by the player’s dominant **Resolve Metric (*Loob*)** tracked across dialogue choices, side-case resolutions, and whether the player prioritized **Preservation (Tala)**, **Autonomy (Dante)**, or **Sacrifice (Mayari)**.

### Ending 1: Ang Bagong Pacto (The New Covenant) — *Canon / True Ending*

* **Trigger Condition:** High *Pakikisama* across all party members, high Preservation score, all 3 district side-cases solved peacefully.
* **Resolution:** Renzo does not slay Bakunawa; he uses the Celestial Chronometer to re-anchor the serpent to its deep slumber while redrawing the *Kasunduan ng Lupa*. The spirits are not banished; they return to their hidden places, but the barrier remains porous enough that mortals must respect the old laws.
* **Epilogue:** Renzo re-opens his grandmother’s shop in Quiapo. It is no longer a dingy front—it is a recognized sanctuary where mortals and spirits come to repair their talismans and settle disputes. Dante runs a legitimate transit network; Tala heads a newly created Ministry of Cultural & Spiritual Heritage; Mayari watches silently from the church bell towers.
* **Closing Image:** Renzo looks down at his watch, smiling faintly. He closes the lid with a satisfying *click*. The hands now tick forward at normal, peaceful speed.

### Ending 2: Ang Nalagot na Kasunduan (The Severed Veil)

* **Trigger Condition:** High Autonomy score (favored Dante's choices), sacrificed ancient relics to upgrade corporate gear.
* **Resolution:** Renzo drives the blade deep, completely severing the connection between the mortal world and the *Kailaliman*. Bakunawa is banished to the outer cosmos, and all supernatural spirits vanish entirely from the archipelago.
* **Epilogue:** Manila returns to mundane reality. The neon signs stop flickering with Baybayin glyphs; the water in the Pasig River is just polluted river water again. Aevum Filipinas collapses without its spirit-energy supply. Renzo returns to being an ordinary citizen, but the city feels hollow, colorless, and sterile. He keeps the broken pocket watch on his desk—it no longer ticks.

### Ending 3: Ang Gabi ng Laho (The Moonless Tide) — *Tragic / Dark Ending*

* **Trigger Condition:** Failed more than 2 major Stagger QTEs during the Bakunawa fight, low party *Pakikisama*.
* **Resolution:** The seal fails to hold. Bakunawa swallows the moon, plunging the Philippine archipelago into eternal nocturnal eclipse. The mortal government collapses as Metro Manila is permanently flooded by the astral tide.
* **Epilogue:** Renzo and the remnants of the coalition become guerrilla survivalists in the rain-drenched ruins of the capital, waging an endless defensive war against the horrors of the Deep Sump under an empty, moonless sky.
