### D. Palit-buhay: bargaining for a life (Episode 2)

Teo "bargaining for Lola's life" fits the canon (the watch's ledger, Sidapa marking lifespans on the balete) and could become a real mechanic.

**Folk grounding (to check with a cultural consultant before it ships):** the *sundô* who comes to fetch the dying. The belief that a life spared is "paid" with another (*may kapalit*). The *palit-ulo* legends about structures that demand lives. The pasiyam and forty-day vigils already in the script.

**Mechanic sketch, "Ang Palit":**
1. **Set the altar.** Choose what to offer from what you're carrying: years (more utang, more white hair), a memory (a remembered moment fades, and a character forgets something you did), a bond (someone trusts you less), or a dead person's keepsake (the ledger, Lolo's panyo, the aswang's tag).
2. **Say the name.** A short timing-ring chant: the full name, three times, on the beat.
3. **The bargain.** A dialogue with the sundô who came for the life, where the tone tags matter (DESPERATE versus RESOLUTE gets different terms).
4. **The price comes due later.** Someone else's watch-time drops, or an NPC Teo helped is now the one being fetched. The ledger never lies.

**Where it goes:** Episode 2 (John's call, 11 Nov). Episode 1 stays as it is at 2:40.


## Part 3: 2.5D exploration (12 Nov)

**John's note:** the side-view walk made Teo look wobbly, and walking only happened at the wake. He wants
exploration like an HD-2D JRPG: the character walks around the place, not just at the wake.

**Why the old walk wobbled:** the four painted frames were not drawn as one cycle. The head moved and the stride
changed size from frame to frame, and frames of different widths were re-centred each step.

**What replaced it:** an explore mode (`godot/scripts/explore_map.gd`), now used by **both hubs**: the wake and the estero.
- **The look.** Small pixel-art sprites standing in a lit 3D diorama, seen from a high, tilted camera that follows Teo.
  The 3D view renders at half resolution and is scaled up without smoothing, so sprites, floor tiles and edges
  share one chunky pixel grid. Candles and lamps are real lights that flicker. Glow, a tilt-shift blur at the top and
  bottom of the frame, a vignette, and drifting dust in the parlor.
- **Teo walks in eight directions** (arrows / WASD / d-pad / stick, or tap the floor) with a 12-frame walk:
  toward the camera, sideways and away, four frames each. All frames sit on one baseline with the head centred,
  so the walk does not jitter. People, furniture, the creek and the walls block him.
- **Talking works as before:** walk up and press SPACE / A, or tap a name tag and he walks over. Saves, checkpoints and
  "who has been heard" are unchanged.
- **The wake** (`data/maps.json` "parlor"): street door at the front left, coffee urn and Mang Domeng, two
  neighbours at the tong-its table, chairs in rows, Aling Nena in the front row, the coffin between candles and
  wreaths, the crucifix, Mang Berto's ghost in the empty chair beside her, the stairs at the back right (the exit).
- **The estero** (`data/maps.json` "estero"): Teo comes in from the street; the creek runs across the map with
  lamplight on the water. The tanod stands on the footbridge, the woman (Tess) sits on the far bank by a step with
  a candle, and the guard waits by the Hiraya sign, the pump house and its fence (the exit).
- **Review links:** add `?map=wake` or `?map=estero` to the game link to open a hub on its own.

**Art.** Generated in OpenArt, then turned into true low-resolution pixel sprites with `tools/pixel_sprites.py`:
Teo's walk sheet, nine people, 12 wake props and 11 estero pieces. Five sheets, about 60 credits. Floors, walls,
wood, concrete and water are drawn in code by `tools/pixel_textures.py`. Credits: `godot/assets/explore/CREDITS.md`.
The estero sheet also drew a sari-sari store, left out because its sign carried real brand logos.

**What is still not walkable:** everything outside the two hubs plays as comic panels, as before. To walk through
the whole episode, each place a scene happens needs its own map. Candidates, in story order:
1. Hidalgo Street outside the parlor, between the cold open and the wake (a short walk to the door).
2. Lola's room upstairs (the watch scene).
3. The street from the parlor to the estero (Act 2, "Follow the water to where it starts").
4. The morgue and Kardo's place (Act 3).
Each one costs about two sprite sheets (people + props) and a `maps.json` entry. **John to pick** which ones,
and whether cutscenes should play *inside* the map (the sprites act out the scene, with panels for the big
beats) or stay as full comic panels.

## Round 4 (Nov 13): Hidalgo Street, acted scenes, the retro test fight

**Hidalgo Street** (`data/maps.json` "hidalgo") is walkable. After the chapter card, Teo walks up the street of
funeral parlors to the Dimaculangan door. On the way he can stop for Mang Isko the balut vendor, Rudy asleep in his
tricycle, and Nonoy staring at the balete (a choice). He has to talk to at least one of them before going in.

**Scenes acted in the map.** A scene beat with a `stage` plays over the map instead of over comic panels. The cast
stands where the beat puts them, and each line can move them (`act`: walk, appear, face, leave, camera focus). A
marker bobs over whoever is speaking. A line that carries a `panel` cuts to that comic panel for the big moment, and
the next line cuts back to the street. Two scenes are acted so far:
- **The arrival:** Teo walks in, and the camera looks over at the neon sign.
- **Kap Obet's visit (`debt_notice`):** Obet walks up the street to the door and Lola comes out. The choice cuts to
  the Obet close-up panel. If Teo throws him out, he walks off.

**The test fight** (`scripts/stage_battle.gd`, `data/stage_enemies.json`) is a retro side-view battle on the street
itself: the enemy on the left, Teo on the right, and blue windows.
- **Time bars:** time bars fill in real time and pause while a menu is open.
- **Teo's commands:**
  - BALISONG: an ordinary cut. Below 30% HP it can turn into BALISONG IKOT, three cuts.
  - HATID: his signature. Pick a prayer, then enter its arrows before the bar runs out. A wrong arrow fizzles it.
  - SALABAT: heals. Two flasks.
  - GUARD: halves the next hit.
- **The enemy, Mang Andoy, a ligaw under the balete:**
  - **Cold Hand:** his ordinary attack, parried on the ring. A perfect parry gives Teo a counter.
  - **TAWAG (the call):** his signature. It glows red a turn ahead. When it comes it can only be dodged, and a hit
    leaves Teo spellbound for a turn. Any prayer landed during the red glow drowns the call out.
  - **The tip:** if you talked to Mang Isko, the warning also tells you that.
- **Two endings:**
  - Below the gold line on his bar, UWI KA NA sends him home. That gives +1 Loob, and Mang Isko remembers it.
  - Otherwise he is scattered. The scene after the fight differs.
- **Where it is:** the fight is optional, on the "Under the balete" tag.
