### B. Characters that actually move in the pictures

The camera move is in. Three ways to go further, cheapest first:

1. **Layered panels (true parallax).** Each key panel is cut into 2–3 layers (background, characters, foreground debris or water), which then slide at different speeds. Uses the same chroma-key cut script as the battle art. About 3 extra images per panel. Best for 5–8 hero moments, not all 60 panels.
2. **Short looping clips (1–3 s).** Image-to-video from the approved panel: rain, flicker, water rising, a ghost's edges smoking. OpenArt can make these. Godot plays `.ogv` clips. I'd test one on the web build before committing, for size and playback.
3. **Animated cutouts on top of the panel.** A character pose from the battle art slides or breathes over a background. Cheap, but it can look like paper puppets.

Recommendation: (2) for 4–6 big beats (the rewind, the aswang drop, the white streak, Lola's tape), plus (1) for the wake.

### D. Palit-buhay: bargaining for a life

Teo "bargaining for Lola's life" fits the canon (the watch's ledger, Sidapa marking lifespans on the balete) and could become a real mechanic.

**Folk grounding (to check with a cultural consultant before it ships):** the *sundô* who comes to fetch the dying. The belief that a life spared is "paid" with another (*may kapalit*). The *palit-ulo* legends about structures that demand lives. The pasiyam and forty-day vigils already in the script.

**Mechanic sketch, "Ang Palit":**
1. **Set the altar.** Choose what to offer from what you're carrying: years (more utang, more white hair), a memory (a remembered moment fades, and a character forgets something you did), a bond (someone trusts you less), or a dead person's keepsake (the ledger, Lolo's panyo, the aswang's tag).
2. **Say the name.** A short timing-ring chant: the full name, three times, on the beat.
3. **The bargain.** A dialogue with the sundô who came for the life, where the tone tags matter (DESPERATE versus RESOLUTE gets different terms).
4. **The price comes due later.** Someone else's watch-time drops, or an NPC Teo helped is now the one being fetched. The ledger never lies.

**Where it would go:** a first, failed version in Episode 1 at "2:40" (Teo tries the old way before Lola is taken: it shows the mechanic and the cost without paying out), and the real version in Episode 2.
