# Comic panel credits

The 26 Episode 1 panels listed below were **made with OpenArt (GPT Image 2.5) on the Starter plan. They are not
cleared for commercial release.** Before any commercial release, check the plan's terms or replace them.

Each source was a 2304×1296 PNG from OpenArt's CDN, resized to 1600×900 (no crop) and saved as webp.
Other files in this folder are older placeholders (see `docs/ASSET_MANIFEST.md`).

Open calls, left as delivered and still John's to decide:
- `ep1_03_lola_packing`: a modern skyline in a 1998 scene.
- `ep1_31_berto_ghost`: the ghost lacks the darkening/smoke edges from the brief.
- `ep1_35_dead_sundo`: the dead sundô looks like Lolo Pido.
- `ep1_15_renzo_cornered`: a plain knife, not a balisong.
