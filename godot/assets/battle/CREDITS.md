# Battle art credits

All Episode 1 battle poses and backgrounds in this folder were **made with OpenArt (GPT Image 2.5) on the Starter plan.
They are not cleared for commercial release.** Before any commercial release, check the plan's terms or replace them.

**Poses** came as sprites facing right on flat #FF00FF. Each was cut out the same way:
- the magenta was keyed out and unmixed from the soft edges, then despilled
- each sheet was split into equal cells, left to right and top row first, and slivers of neighbouring cells were dropped
- the figure was trimmed and fitted to its canvas, feet at the bottom centre: party 440x680, enemies 600x560

Each character keeps one scale across all its poses, so the figure doesn't jump in size between moves. The idle images
came separately and were matched in height to the standing pose on the same character's sheet.

**Backgrounds** were 2304x1296 PNGs, resized to 1600x900 (no crop) and saved as webp.

**Bonus poses** are saved but nothing in the game calls them yet: `lunod/defeat`, `aswang/defeat`, `aswang/windup_lunge`,
`hiraya_hound/defeat` and `hiraya_hound/windup_burst`.

Source images: `https://cdn.openart.ai/openart-ai/production/2026-11/create-image/VmKWQUD0VaN1RAcbdWVV/gpt-image-2.5-sunburst-edit-1_<code>.png`
