# Storylines — BALETE: Echoes of the Sunken Veil (Game Intro)

Goal: Open the game like a classic *Final Fantasy* title sequence — building the mythic Quiapo lore, family debt, and supernatural combat stakes out of the repository's own comic plates before swelling into the playable-feeling title emblem.

## 1. "Four Minutes, Seventeen Seconds" — The Borrowed-Time Overture *(Recommended)*

**Why recommended:** It traces one real object—Lolo Pido's balete pocket watch—from the 1998 flood that broke Quiapo to the moment Teo inherits it, landing directly on the game's actual `title_keyart.webp` and brass sundô seal.

- **Device & Form:** Object-to-object chain (stopped clocks → pocket watch → black floodwater → Magwayen's boat → Teo's watch → title seal) with bar-locked cuts at 80 BPM, accelerating into 2-beat combat flashes before a breathless drop into the title reveal.
- **First 2 seconds:** Frame 0 opens on the parlor wall of nine clocks stopped at 4:17 (`ep1_02b_stopped_clocks.webp`) under warm amber light as a brass clock-hand overlay ticks backward to 4:17 and the caption `QUIAPO · 3 JUNE 1998 · 4:17 AM` sharpens into view.
- **Beats:** `Hook (4:17 clocks) → Flood & Rewind (Pido) → Downriver Price (Santa Ana) → The Calling (Wake & Magwayen's Boat) → Escalation Montage (Estero, Tagged Aswang, Balisong, White Streak) → The Heir (Watch & 47 Days) → Final Fantasy Title Reveal (title_keyart.webp + Sundô Seal + BALETE)`.
- **Look:** Midnight ink `#050814`, warm candle gold `#e8c872` / `#ffd27a`, bone `#f4f2ea`, single supernatural teal `#00ffcc` / `#8fe3ff`; repo fonts `Anton` + `Oswald` + `Barlow`; graphic-novel ink+cel plates with subtle grain and radial vignette.
- **Sound:** Scott Buckley's *Aphelion* (`godot/assets/bgm/aphelion.ogg`, 80 BPM, starting at `144.765 s` so its quiet dip lands under the 47-days watch hold and its `174 s` peak strikes the `BALETE` reveal), layered with `rain-loop.ogg` and repo SFX (`hit-thud-metal.ogg`, `command-enter-pitchdrop.mp3`, `extra-chime.mp3`, `dodge-swish-1.wav`, `dodge-swish-9.wav`, `slash-knife.ogg`, `command-exit-riser.mp3`, `stagger-break-3tone.mp3`).

## 2. "The Sundô's Seal" — Ensemble Cast Overture

- **Device & Form:** Match cuts rotating around the circular pocket watch dial (`ep1_25_watch_future.webp`), introducing Teo, Lola Ising, Kardo, Lolo Pido, Mang Berto, and Celeste like an ensemble *Final Fantasy VI / XVI* character-parade opening.
- **First 2 seconds:** Tight close-up on Teo's hand raising the teal-lit balete pocket watch against Quiapo Church (`ep1_25_watch_future.webp`), hands spinning backward.
- **Beats:** `Watch Dial → Lolo Pido (1998) → Lola Ising (Stairs) → Kardo (Truck) → Teo vs. Aswang → Celeste (TV) → Title Emblem`.
- **Look:** Midnight-and-amber split-band portrait frames with `Anton` character cards and teal accents.
- **Sound:** *Undertow* (`godot/assets/bgm/undertow.ogg`) driving a tense nocturnal pulse into a chime impact on the title card.

## 3. "Abangan: The Night Shift" — Rain-First Teleserye-Meets-JRPG Cold Open *(Bold)*

- **Device & Form:** Opens in near-silence with only Quiapo rain (`rain-loop.ogg`) over the parlor doorway (`title_keyart.webp`), cuts on thunder/stagger impacts through Episode 1's wake and estero, then frames the title inside the game's SNES/PS1 cobalt gradient JRPG menu window (`Retro.window`).
- **First 2 seconds:** Rain streaks over `title_keyart.webp` with only the Baybayin glyph `ᜊᜎᜒᜆᜒ` and *"They bury the dead by day."*
- **Beats:** `Rain Opening → Wake Ghost → Ferrywoman → Estero Pump → White Streak → JRPG Blue-Window Title Screen`.
- **Look:** Deep JRPG cobalt `#1c2b85`→`#080d33` menu window with `#e0e6f7` borders over dark comic plates.
- **Sound:** 6 seconds of pure rain bed, then *Aphelion* entering abruptly as Magwayen's lantern boat appears in the doorway.
